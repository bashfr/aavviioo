Teaching

Since July 2026, I have been working as a teacher at a private college, although I have no formal education in this field. It happened completely by accident, but I am still infinitely glad that I have the opportunity to try myself in a place other than classical IT: instead of writing programs or setting up computer networks, I give students an understanding of how programs and computers work, while consolidating what is being told inside themselves.

The reason for this transformation is simple: I don't mentally pull out 8-hour jobs in the office for constant development or adjustment of what seems to me pointless, if not delusional, which is why the weekend was spent on recovery to the detriment of household chores.

Why I teaching:

  1. Keep motivated to study the field of software development, from modern high-level languages to fundamental low-level processes.
  2. To explain in more or less understandable words what I have learned and tried myself to the students.
  3. Have the finances and time to refine personal life and develop my own projects.

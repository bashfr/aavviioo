Bad works

Here are the works that I personally find to be bad, and describe why this feeling arises. These may be finished works or drafts of them that will never be completed.

My subjective verdict does not mean that the works listed below are really bad. It is quite possible that they did not fit my ideas or interests about composition and genre.

Audio

A small remark: the mastering of these tracks has not been completed, their sound may vary on different devices.

Repeating pattern

This problem with composing music has been haunting me for the last 4 years. Almost all tracks from 2023 to the present are recorded in a 4/4 grid (standard in DAW).

I know music theory very poorly, which played a cruel trick on me when trying to delve deeper into a musical composition and try to make the works more dynamic. That's probably why I moved away from hard-coded notes and instruments to improvisation: they sound more natural, more human.

“Boring” structure

The compositions differ in terms of instrumentation, but each of them displays the same, “boring” pattern: unnaturally clear, carried over from project to project. The lack of dynamics distances the listener’s attention, which is not very good if the genre of the work implies engagement with the musical process.
